## 1.0.0 (2026-09-23)

### Features

* Import a Garmin workout `.fit` plan, validated and expanded on the device
* Step-by-step workout runner with countdown dial, −10s, skip and pause
* Streak, month calendar and recent sessions on the Dashboard
* Audio and haptic cues on every new step and at the finish
