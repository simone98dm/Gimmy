# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

A solo lifter who builds workouts in Garmin Connect and trains alone in a gym. The phone sits on the floor or a bench, glanced at between sets from a metre or two away, with a raised heart rate and sweaty hands. The job: follow the plan step by step without thinking about the app, and see afterwards that the day counted.

## Product Purpose

gimmy imports a Garmin `.fit` workout plan, runs it one step at a time (timers, reps, rest), and keeps a streak and a calendar of past sessions. It exists so a plan built in Garmin Connect can be run from a phone. Success is a user who opens the app, taps Start, and finishes the workout without ever fighting the interface.

## Positioning

Runs your Garmin-built workout on your phone, fully local: no signup, no cloud, no subscription.

## Brand Personality

Focused, honest, tough. Focused: it gets out of the way mid-set and shows only what the current step needs. Honest: it never inflates what you did, and it says exactly what was saved and what counts. Tough: built for sweat, chalk, and a phone on a gym floor, not for a desk.

## Anti-references

- Subscription fitness apps: paywalls, account walls, social feeds, upsells.
- Garmin Connect clutter: dense menus, every metric at once, settings sprawl.

## Design Principles

- **Readable mid-set.** The thing you need right now is the biggest thing on screen, and the control you need next is the biggest thing you can touch.
- **Say what's true.** Copy reports exactly what was saved and counted; no celebration that wasn't earned, no silence about what was lost.
- **Yours, offline.** Nothing asks for an account or a connection; the plan and the history live on the device.
- **Nothing in the way.** Every screen serves one task; secondary detail waits until it's asked for.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Not yet set as a target by the owner. Current practice in the code: WCAG AA contrast enforced by a test in both themes and brightnesses, 44dp minimum touch targets, the OS reduce-motion setting honoured by every animation, and unclamped text scaling.
